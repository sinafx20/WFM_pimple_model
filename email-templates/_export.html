How this works: the thumbnail ({{thumb}}), firm logo ({{logo}}), brand colour ({{brand_color}}), presenter ({{presenter}}/{{presenter_title}}), booking ({{booking}}) and tool link ({{volcano_blob}}) are all per-lead custom variables the studio pushes into Instantly. You just: (1) paste each Subject + HTML into the campaign step; (2) set the send-day delays; (3) enable Instantly unsubscribe (replaces [[ unsubscribe ]]); (4) subjects use spintax {a|b} in the subject field; (5) test one live send to confirm merge vars substitute raw (not URL-encoded).
